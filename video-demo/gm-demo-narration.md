# Morelord Marketplace — GM demo narration

The timestamps target `MorelordMarketplace-GMDemo-Branded.mp4`.

## 00:00–00:05 — Opening

Morelord Marketplace brings flexible, immersive shopping directly into your Foundry VTT world.

## 00:05–00:21 — Global configuration

Game Masters choose how the global marketplace behaves. Enable buying, selling, or catalog browsing independently, set the value players receive when selling gear, and choose which compendiums supply the catalog.

## 00:21–00:56 — Global marketplace

Players shop as their active character and always see the currency available to them. The marketplace combines fast text search with detailed, tri-state filters, making large compendium libraries easy to explore. Results can be narrowed by item type, category, rarity, source, price, weapon class, weapon range, properties, masteries, and attunement. Players can also limit the list to items they can afford.

## 00:56–00:59 — Vendor chapter

Premium Shop Manager tools turn a catalog into a living part of the world.

## 00:59–01:29 — Shop prefabs

Start with a ready-made vendor prefab or manage an existing store. Each shop has its own identity and access rules, with independent controls for buying and selling. A token image gives the vendor a recognizable presence on the scene.

## 01:29–02:07 — Products and stock

Choose the products and rarities the shop can offer, then shape its economy with reputation-aware pricing. Shops can carry unlimited inventory, limited randomized stock, or a hybrid of both. Restocking rules let inventory refresh automatically while still giving the Game Master direct control.

## 02:07–02:10 — Player chapter

Once the vendor is ready, place it on the scene and let the party shop in character.

## 02:10–02:48 — Shopping context

Players can choose who receives purchased equipment and which character or shared group actor pays. The cart tracks stock, purchase totals, and the party's remaining currency before anything is committed. Limited-stock reservations and revision checks help prevent conflicting purchases between open sessions.

## 02:48–03:17 — Purchase flow

Every item keeps its artwork, rarity, source, and compendium link close at hand. When the player is ready, Morelord Marketplace handles the currency and inventory transfer, while optional approval workflows can keep the Game Master in control of global transactions.

## 03:17–03:23 — Closing

Morelord Marketplace. Because every adventure has a price.
