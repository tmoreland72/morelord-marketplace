# Optional narration

Morelord Marketplace brings buying, selling, and vendor management directly into your Foundry world.

Players can browse a global catalog, sell from their own inventory, and see exactly what their character can afford. Powerful filters make even the largest compendiums quick to explore.

For a more immersive game, turn scene tokens into real vendors. Each shop can have its own products, stock, pricing, reputation, and restocking rules. Players choose who receives the gear and whether a character or shared party actor pays.

Game Masters can start from ready-made shop templates, customize every shelf, and optionally review player transactions before they are completed.

Morelord Marketplace: because every adventure has a price.
