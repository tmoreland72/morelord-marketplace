## Marketplace 0.10.0 is here: share your gear!

Found a weapon your teammate needs? Want to move supplies into the party's shared inventory? Marketplace now has a **Transfer** tab.

Add an item or a whole stack to the Shopping Cart, adjust the quantity with **+ / −**, choose another player character or a Group, and click **Transfer Items**. The inventories update automatically, and a chat receipt shows who received what. No gold changes hands and no approval is needed—just have a GM connected. Empty containers before transferring them.

We've also given Marketplace a cleaner layout: proper tabs, roomier carts across Buy/Sell/Transfer, collapsible Buy filters, and tidy Source labels. Transfer receipts use the same style as other Marketplace transactions.

**Update both Morelord Core (0.4.0) and Marketplace (0.10.0), then reload your world.** Transfers are included in the standard Marketplace; no premium access is required.
